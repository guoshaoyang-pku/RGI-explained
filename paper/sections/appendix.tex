\section{Reproducibility details}

\paragraph{Data and model hashes.}
The FineWeb source is \texttt{HuggingFaceFW/fineweb}, configuration \texttt{sample-10BT}, revision prefix \texttt{9bb295dd}, shard \texttt{sample/10BT/001\_00000.parquet}.  The pool selection starts after source row 65,536 and uses selection seed 610000 and split seed 610001.  The verified tokenizer commit begins \texttt{a39f36b1}.  Full hashes and input manifests are in \url{analysis/rgi_extension_20261006/new-provenance.json} and the accompanying evidence manifest in the research workspace.

\paragraph{Evaluation units.}
Natural batches contain 8 documents and 223 scored next-token targets per document (query positions 32 through 254).  Difficulty and response statistics are computed from document-level means; token positions within a document are not treated as independent documents.  The known-teacher continuation uses 33 natural seed tokens followed by 63 sampled tokens and evaluates 63 targets per generated document.

\paragraph{Verification scope.}
Each of the three common-start protocols completed all 18 runs and passed 2,278 independent checks, for 6,834 total.  The respective maximum numerical errors are \(3.35\times10^{-13}\), \(9.59\times10^{-14}\), and \(2.39\times10^{-14}\).  NumPy reconstructs the final eight-token full-vocabulary fixtures independently, and checks zero initial terms, paired document difficulty, fixed-probe difficulty, schedules, hashes, response identities, and JVP mean parity.  All 54 fixtures were harvested with hash verification.  The checker does not independently replay network gradients or training.  A separate CPU FP64 real-model pulse agrees between gradient-dot and JVP work to \(6.25\times10^{-16}\), providing numerical engineering evidence rather than an independent full training replication.

The original protocol ran on A100 with PyTorch 2.7.0; the two rate extensions ran on H200 with PyTorch 2.9.1.  Comparisons are paired within each protocol.  Checkpoint canonical tensor bytes match across hosts; small cross-host operation differences are not assumed absent.

\paragraph{Additional diagnostics.}
The registered 64-step block analysis uses signed covariance shares; these shares are attribution coefficients and may exceed one or be negative because terms cancel.  High-pass SGD failures and the optimizer-difference failure are retained in the main text rather than averaged away.  The three permutations reuse one data pool and are not independent datasets.

\paragraph{Code and artifacts.}
The arXiv source package includes the central table, four-term curves, retained failed rate protocols, MetaCircle assets, and compiled bibliography.  The public project provides compact numerical evidence, protocol contracts, independent checker and plotting code.  The release manifest identifies which raw weights, corpus spans, and full logit fixtures are omitted; a saved-evidence audit is distinct from rerunning training.
